Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Martins & Associates Real Estate (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit this website or contact us through it.

Who we are

Martins & Associates Real Estate is a residential brokerage licensed in Massachusetts and Rhode Island, based in Brockton, Massachusetts. You can reach us at martinsrealestate@msn.com or (508) 583-6060.

Information we collect

When you use our contact or listing inquiry forms, we collect the details you submit, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Your message, including preferred times or a property address
  • The service you selected, such as a showing or a general inquiry

We also store your language preference in your browser’s local storage so the site can remember your choice. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and similar infrastructure may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, and request logs in order to deliver and secure the site. Fonts and images served by third parties (including Google Fonts and image hosts) may receive your IP address when your browser requests those files.

How we use information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule showings or consultations
  • Communicate about listings and real estate services you asked about
  • Operate, secure, and improve this website
  • Meet legal, licensing, and professional recordkeeping obligations

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with our agents and staff who need it to help you; with service providers who process form submissions and email (currently Cloudflare’s email service) so we can receive your message; when required by law or to protect our rights and clients; and with referral partners only if you ask us to connect you with them, such as for an international referral.

Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. We store your language choice in local storage on your device. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep information

Inquiry emails and related records are kept as long as needed to respond to you, provide real estate services, and meet recordkeeping, licensing, and legal requirements.

Your choices

You may email us to ask what information we have about you, request a correction, or ask us to stop using your contact details for follow-up. We may still keep records we are required to keep. If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information sent through this site, including transmitting form submissions over HTTPS. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date above.

Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email martinsrealestate@msn.com or call (508) 583-6060.
